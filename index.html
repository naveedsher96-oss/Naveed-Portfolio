CRM automation from A to Z

One person owns it end to end, so nothing gets lost between "strategy" and "setup".

Audit

A short call and a look inside your current tools. Where do leads come from, where do they get stuck, and what do you do by hand every week?

Map

A simple diagram of your customer journey: stages, triggers, messages and who owns each step. You approve it before I build.

Build

Pipelines, custom fields, forms, calendars, workflows, email and SMS templates, written in your voice.

Connect

Website, ads, phone, payments and calendars wired in, plus your old contacts imported and cleaned.

Test

CO

Coaches & consultants

Discovery calls to paid programs

Problem: leads from Instagram and webinars go cold, discovery calls no-show, and payments and onboarding are handled by hand.

  1. Lead magnet or webinar form tags the contact by source and interest
  2. Instant email and SMS with the free resource and a booking link
  3. Discovery call reminders at 24 hours and 1 hour, with a no-show rebooking sequence
  4. After the call, the coach picks an outcome and the right proposal or nurture path starts
  5. Stripe payment triggers the welcome email, client portal access and session scheduling
  6. Mid-program check-in, testimonial request at the end, and a renewal offer
GoHighLevelStripeZoomMemberships
Goal: more booked calls from the same traffic, fewer no-shows, and onboarding that runs itself.
TH

Therapists & counselors

Private practice intake

Problem: inquiries come in after hours, intake paperwork is chased by email, and missed sessions are lost income.

  1. Website inquiry or missed call gets a warm, immediate reply with next steps
  2. Consultation booking with insurance or self-pay questions asked up front
  3. Intake forms and consent sent automatically, with reminders until they're done
  4. Session reminders by SMS and email, plus a cancellation link that reopens the slot
  5. Recurring session scheduling and a gentle re-engagement message after a gap
  6. Clinical notes stay in the practice's own system, never in marketing messages
GoHighLevel or ZohoSecure formsCalendarSMS
Goal: no lost inquiries, intake finished before the first session, and fewer late cancellations.
RE

Real estate teams

Speed to lead and long nurture

Problem: portal and ad leads wait hours for a call, and buyers who aren't ready yet are forgotten.

  1. Leads from Zillow, Facebook and the website land in one pipeline
  2. Round-robin assignment with an instant text, and the agent is alerted
  3. If nobody calls within 5 minutes, the lead is reassigned
  4. Buyer and seller tracks with property alerts and market updates
  5. Long-term nurture for 6 to 12 month buyers, with past clients asked for referrals
Bitrix24 or GHLFacebook Lead AdsRound-robin
Goal: every lead contacted in minutes and every agent's pipeline visible in one place.
MS

Dental, med spa & clinics

Bookings, recalls and reviews

Problem: the front desk misses calls, patients forget recalls, and happy patients never leave reviews.

  1. Missed-call text-back and a website chat that books straight into the calendar
  2. New patient forms sent before the visit
  3. Recall reminders at 6 months, or treatment-specific follow-ups for spa services
  4. Post-visit message asking for a Google review, with unhappy replies routed to the manager
  5. Reactivation campaign for patients not seen in 12 months
GoHighLevelReputationChat widgetSMS
Goal: fuller schedules from existing patients and a steady stream of new reviews.
FIT

Gyms & fitness studios

Trial to membership

Problem: free-trial signups don't show up or don't convert, and members quietly cancel.

  1. Trial pass signup triggers a welcome message and class booking
  2. Reminder before the first visit and a check-in right after it
  3. Day 5 membership offer with a deadline, then a follow-up from a coach
  4. Attendance drop alert sends a "we miss you" message and flags the coach
  5. Birthday and milestone messages, plus a member referral reward
GHL or HubSpotStripeCalendar
Goal: more trials turning into members and fewer silent cancellations.
B2B

Agencies & B2B services

Outbound to signed contract

Problem: outreach lives in spreadsheets, proposals get no follow-up, and nobody knows the real pipeline value.

  1. Prospect lists imported, enriched and assigned to reps
  2. Multi-step email and call sequence with tasks created for each rep
  3. Replies and booked meetings move the deal stage automatically
  4. Proposal sent starts a follow-up timer and alerts the owner if it goes quiet
  5. Won deals trigger client onboarding tasks; weekly pipeline report emailed to leadership
Zoho CRMHubSpotZoho CampaignsMake
Goal: a pipeline you can trust and follow-ups that happen without reminders.
