What changes

Your week, before and after.

The same leads and the same team. The difference is what happens automatically in between.

Before

  • Leads wait hours (or days) for a first reply
  • Follow-up depends on someone remembering
  • Missed calls go to voicemail and never call back
  • No-shows leave empty slots in the calendar
  • Happy customers never leave a review
  • Nobody knows which ad or channel actually pays

After

  • Every lead gets a personal reply in under a minute
  • Multi-step follow-up runs until they book or say no
  • Missed calls get an instant text and a callback task
  • Reminders and easy rebooking cut no-shows
  • Review requests go out after every good visit
  • A simple dashboard shows leads, bookings and revenue by source

FAQ

Questions clients ask.

Which CRM should I use?

It depends on your business. GoHighLevel suits local businesses, coaches and agencies that want SMS, funnels and booking in one place. Zoho and HubSpot suit B2B teams with longer sales cycles. Bitrix24 suits teams that also want tasks and internal chat. I'll recommend one on the free call.

Can you fix the CRM I already have?

Yes, and it's often the fastest win. I clean up contacts, remove broken workflows, simplify the pipeline and add the automations that are missing.

How do you charge?

A fixed price per project after the blueprint, so you know the cost up front. Ongoing support and small changes can be hourly or a monthly retainer.

Do I need to give you my passwords?

No. You add me as a user in your account, and you can remove me at any time. Everything is built in your account and stays yours.

Can you write the messages too?

Yes. With a sales background, writing follow-ups that get replies is part of the job. You review and approve every template before launch.

What about time zones?

I'm in GMT+5 and overlap with European working hours and US-East mornings. Updates are written, so nothing depends on being online at the same time.
